## Packaging

`package-frames.mjs` identifies the 12 alpha-connected figures in each sheet, isolates each from neighboring figures with a small expanded component mask, then crops, scales and places it on a shared 256×320 canvas. This is sprite extraction; it does not redraw or recolor artwork. Source RGB and generated alpha are retained. Lossless WebP output avoids chromatic compression. Tiny nonneutral antialias pixels from the generator remain: the player must use `filter: grayscale(1)` to guarantee neutral display. There is no painted colored region. White interiors remain visible against interface cards, allowing genuine occlusion.
