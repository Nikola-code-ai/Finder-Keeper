# Manga shop background

Created with the built-in image generation tool. The final original source is `cozy-source.png`; runtime asset: `public/scenes/manga-bookshop-cozy.webp`. Packaging is WebP encoding at quality 90 only; artwork was not programmatically retouched.

## Initial prompt

Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: full-page background for an interactive black-and-white manga bookshop.
Primary request: the inside of a real, small, lived-in secondhand bookshop, drawn as a quiet establishing panel from a mature slice-of-life seinen manga. The viewer is a customer just inside the shop.
Composition: wide landscape, 3:2. Eye-level, believable modest room perspective. Tall wooden bookcases packed with uneven old book spines run down the left side and across the upper rear wall; lower shelves, a few casually leaning volumes, a small rolling wooden ladder, exposed timber ceiling beams, and a simple old window on the far right. Clearly readable bookshop architecture, hand-drawn joinery, worn floorboards at the bottom. The lower central half is a calm open working area for a real interactive search counter and drawers that will be layered over this image. Keep the central lower area relatively light and uncluttered; concentrate detailed shelves, books and crosshatching along the sides and upper portion. Do not draw a foreground counter or foreground furniture across the middle, because the functioning interface itself will be the counter.
Style: black pen and dip-pen ink on slightly imperfect white manga paper; visible irregular line weight, spontaneous small hatch marks, dry-brush black accents, sparse printed screentone. Natural human drawing, slightly rough, with restrained detail. Warm and welcoming through composition, strictly monochrome black/white/gray. Clear lines at medium contrast, especially shelving, not washed away into mist. No polished digital painting, smooth airbrush, photorealism, 3D render, vector precision or sepia.
Constraints: no people or animals (two existing animated characters will be layered in later), no lettering, no words, no signage, no logos, no UI controls, no speech bubbles, no borders around the image, no artificial spotlight. Attractive believable small shop, quiet rather than cluttered.

## Cozy revision prompt

Edit this manga bookshop background to feel OLDER, COZIER, A LITTLE UNKEMPT BUT CHARMING. Preserve its room perspective and broad layout (shelves left and rear, window right, open floor and working space at lower center). Make the shop feel lovingly inhabited over decades rather than carefully staged: slightly sagging and crooked wooden shelves, uneven leaning stacks mixed with gaps, a few battered books stacked horizontally in the corners, worn rounded shelf edges, an old cushioned wooden chair tucked against the left shelving, a small mismatched desk lamp on a right-side shelf, two loosely curled papers tucked beside books, a slightly crooked small framed sketch, trailing plant growth. Add three fine modest cobwebs in upper shelf corners and near the window where a friendly spider would live. No spider or other character in this background, since our animated spider is layered separately. The mess should be gentle and appealing, not trash, abandonment, decay, spooky or dirty. Let pale window light make the wood-and-paper room inviting, STRICTLY BLACK AND WHITE, no warm-colored tint. Keep the lower center uncluttered for the functional counter UI. Draw it like an actual lightly rough seinen manga panel: expressive imperfect ink, varied outlines, selective hatch strokes and screentone; reduce overly uniform microscopic book detailing and avoid a photograph converted to sketch. No text, letters, signs, UI, people, animals, speech bubbles or foreground counter. Keep all charm in the environment itself.
